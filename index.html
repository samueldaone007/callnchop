Who is doing the cooking

Ada Nwosu tried the double fry
at home for a month

She could not make the skin come out the same twice, so she stopped pretending it was a recipe and started paying attention to the oil. First fry at 190°, ten minutes uncovered, second at 205°. That rest is the whole difference between chicken that stays juicy and chicken that turns to rubber, and it is the reason a whole bird is 55 minutes on this site rather than 30.

She opened with two burners in March 2024 and is on her fourth fryer now. Five cooks, one cashier, and a Saturday peak of 186 birds. She still drops the Saturday birds herself, which is the only reason the timings on this page are honest.

How to read the chips on the menu

Contains Gluten
In the crumb or the flour. We fry in shared oil, so we cannot promise a crumb-free plate even if the dish does not list it.
Contains Groundnut
Suya, in the suya spice and on the rotisserie rub. Serious reactions are not something we will freestyle on.
Vegan
No animal product in the dish itself. Ask about the fryer oil if this matters, because we do change it during service.
Bone-in
The bird is not jointed for you. If that is a problem, take the tenders, and tell us so you do not get them by accident.
Very hot
Heat three of three. There is no mild version and we will not make one. Suya chicken is the survivable alternative.
Chef’s pick
What Ada would order if you handed her the till for a night and told her to stop making bad decisions.

We do not label anything allergen-free and we will not guess. If you are severe, WhatsApp the counter before you order and Ada will tell you what the fryer was doing at that moment in the day.
